# Kindlehold — Player Guide

## The story

Seven winters after the Long Frost, the Hearthbound return to Kindlehold, a burnt
hearth-keep in the Harrowmere valley. Maren Ashgrove, the Lantern Warden, leads them.
Across the river, the Rustfang toll-raiders of Vharek the Tollbreaker hold the old ford
fort and will not share the valley.

## Your first ten minutes

1. **Rekindle the hearth.** Left-click the round stone Keep, then press *Rekindle the
   Hearth* in the command panel (bottom right). Settlers only come home to a burning hearth.
2. **Timber and food.** Press **B** (or *Build…* at the Keep). Place a *Woodcutter's Lodge*
   near the western trees (it needs trees within 28 m — the ghost turns green when a spot
   is valid) and a *Farmstead* on open grass. Your labourers carry the materials from the
   Keep and build.
3. **Housing.** Each *Cottage* houses five people. New settlers arrive about every 16 s
   while there is free housing and the store has provisions.
4. **Stone and iron.** A *Quarry* needs rock outcrops (south-east of the Keep), an *Iron
   Mine* must stand within 10 m of the iron vein under the western hill. Miners eat one
   provision for every two iron.
5. **Arms.** Build the *Barracks* and train soldiers. Each soldier is one of your settlers.

## Reading your settlement

* **Top-left ribbon:** stores in the Keep with the change over the last minute,
  people / housing, labourers who are free to haul and build, and stability.
* **Stability** (the flame bar) rises when everyone is fed and housed. Hunger and burnt
  buildings lower it. Low stability slows all work; below 30 no one new arrives.
* **Select a building** to see its workers, stock, fields, and — in amber — exactly why
  it has stopped: *no worker*, *storage full* (labourers are busy; you need more people),
  *waiting for provisions*, *nothing left in range*, or *cannot be reached*.
* **Objectives** (top right) list what to do next; blue boxes are tutorial hints
  (switch off in Settings → Gameplay). Buttons that matter right now glow.

## Research (at the Keep, one at a time)

| Study | Effect |
|---|---|
| Keen Axes | Foresters chop 35% faster |
| Braced Timber | Build and repair 40% faster; Cottages cost less timber |
| Tempered Blades | Soldiers deal 25% more damage (needs a Barracks) |
| March Charter | +6 housing, bigger territory, unlocks Watchtowers (needs Braced Timber) |

## Fighting

* **Shieldbearers** beat blades, **Bladesmen** beat archers and slingers, **Fletchers**
  beat shield lines. Mixed groups fight best: select them together and right-click — the
  formation puts shields in front and archers behind.
* **A** attack-move (fight on the way), **P** patrol, **S** stop, **H** hold position.
  **Ctrl+1–9** stores a group; **1–9** recalls it.
* **Maren Ashgrove:** allies within 10 m heal slowly and take 10% less damage.
  **F — Beacon Flare** bursts her lantern (40 damage, enemies attack much slower for 5 s).
  **G — Kindle the Line** wards nearby allies (absorbs 60 damage) and speeds them up.
  If she falls she recovers at the Keep after 40 s.
* **Watchtowers** shoot raiders within 16 m.

## Winning and losing

Survive the Rustfang raid (a scout warns you two minutes ahead), then cross the river at
the Tollford and destroy the **Warhall** at the ford fort. Vharek defends it when it is
threatened. If the Keep is destroyed — or everyone is gone — the scenario is lost.

## Saving

**F5** quick-saves, **F9** quick-loads; the pause menu (**Esc**) has three save slots.
The game autosaves every two minutes. Saves are stored in this browser only.
